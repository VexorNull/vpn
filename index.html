Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does VexorVPN unblock websites?

VexorVPN acts as an encrypted secure intermediary proxy gateway. When you enter a URL, our proxy nodes fetch the content and securely render it through a bypass tunnel, hiding your real IP address from local network firewalls.

Will this work on blocked school or office WiFi?

Yes! Because VexorVPN routes requests via high-speed external global node clusters, it bypasses local DNS filters and deep packet inspection (DPI) firewalls instantly.

Is my data logged or tracked?

Never. VexorVPN strictly adheres to a Zero-Logs policy. Created by Tanveer Hussain (@vexornull), all proxy requests are ephemeral and discarded immediately after delivery.
